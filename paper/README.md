# Paper

`paper.pdf` is a short write-up of the tool and its evaluation: "Rules First, Models Second:
Detecting Personal Data in English, French, German and Luxembourgish Text".

`main.tex` and `references.bib` are the sources. They compile with `latexmk -pdf main.tex` or on
Overleaf (New Project, Upload Project). Every number in the paper comes from the result files in
[`eval/results/`](../eval/results/).
